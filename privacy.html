Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to n8n-auto-upload.

n8n-auto-upload is a web application that enables users to upload video content to TikTok using TikTok’s official API.

Information We Collect

  • We process TikTok account authorization data through OAuth.
  • We may temporarily process video data for upload purposes.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users via TikTok OAuth.
  • To upload video content to TikTok on behalf of the user.

Data Storage

We do not permanently store personal data or video content.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data with third parties.

User Rights

Users can revoke access at any time via TikTok account settings.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data.
